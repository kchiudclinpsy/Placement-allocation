What works

Validation gate
Required columns, IDs, references, ranks, weights and capacity are checked before optimization.
Hard constraints
Capacity, car access, specialty requirements and travel limits cannot be violated.
Transparent scoring
Student choice, provider ranking, fairness and travel fit are shown separately in the CSV outputs.
Decision alternatives
Three feasible options expose who benefits or loses when a contested place changes.
Audit trail
Inputs, validation, results, summary and options can be retained together for review.
Human review remains essential
The model supports decisions; it should not automatically resolve exceptional circumstances or equality issues.
